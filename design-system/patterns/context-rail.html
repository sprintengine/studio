Model pickers

Content starts three columns in. Each one is a list of things to choose, so nothing says which choice the screen is actually about.

Three navigation columns before the first word of content. A surface that seems to need two levels of rail folds the outer level into a group of the one rail, as above — never into a column of its own.

Anatomy

  • Brand row. Fixed above the rail. Outside the swap.
  • Create + narrow. The surface's New … row and its search/filter lead the rail and stay pinned while the list scrolls — the same anatomy on every surface, so there is one place to look.
  • Groups. One list, or several under quiet headings. A heading earns its place only by separating one group from another: over a lone list, or over a list whose search field already names it, it is the same word said twice.
  • Back. A rail row pinned to the bottom, below everything. It restores the rail the surface replaced, and Escape does the same. The canvas carries no back affordance — two affordances are two answers to one question.

Selection in a two-level rail

  • The rail keeps one focused selection, at bg.selected: the thing the canvas is showing.
  • A group carrying outer context — which horizon, which project, which run — rests at bg.selected-resting permanently, whether or not the rail holds focus. It answers "where am I?", and it must not compete with "what am I looking at?".
  • Mark the group, never infer it from position: which group is the outer one belongs to the surface.

Motion

  • The incoming rail slides in from its leading edge over motion.duration.normal on motion.ease.standard — opacity and one transform, nothing that reflows.
  • Replacement in the same column at the same width is precisely the change a hard cut reads as a glitch: without the slide, everything in the column changes at once with nothing to say it arrived.
  • Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the treatment drops out entirely. A shortened slide is a stutter.
