List surface

The anatomy every rail, column, and panel whose job is “find a thing in a list” is built from. Create, then search with one filter glyph, then one divider, then the rows. Tokens and components alone never caught the defects this pattern exists to prevent — they are all composition defects.

When this surface is the list inside a rail that replaces a column — a door rail rather than a standalone panel — its row belongs to a shared alignment grid, because several rails take turns in that one column. The row numbers here (.rail-rows 4px, .rail-row 6/8px, title body over meta meta) are the ones that grid adopts; the rest of it — scrollport, leading icon slot, Back row, and the 36px title edge they add up to — is ruled in context-rail under "The alignment grid".
