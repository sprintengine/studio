Selection

Selection answers “what did I pick?”. Focus answers “where am I typing?”. They are different questions and never share a treatment. Selection is a neutral fill in two tiers — the accent is reserved for the primary action, so a chosen row never outranks the one thing worth clicking.

Three panes shouting at once, each with an accent fill and a left bar. Nothing says which list has focus, three signals carry one state, and the accent no longer means “this is the action”. Selection never uses the accent, and never carries a left bar.

Where a left bar is still correct

Only on a collapsed icon rail, where rows are too narrow for a fill to read. Everywhere else — every content list — selection is a fill.
