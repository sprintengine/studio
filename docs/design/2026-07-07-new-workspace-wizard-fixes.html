What changed vs the shipped wizard

  • Footer pinned outside the scroll region. In NewWorkspacePanel.tsx the hint + Continue div is the last child of the scrolling <main> column; here it is a shrink-0 sibling of <main> inside the panel's flex column, so Continue never leaves the viewport on tall steps (roster, guided-idea, multiloop-goal, embedded chat composer).
  • The decorative logo block is gone. The logo-rise 64px mark + wordmark above the heading spent ~140px of the first step's viewport; brand identity moves to a small mark in the header strip.
  • The form rides a real surface. Inputs sat directly on the wallpaper with only a radial scrim for contrast; here the step content is a single --bg-surface card with a hairline border and --shadow-drawer, matching the Settings overlay's material quality. Recent rows live on --bg-surface-raised.
  • Recent mode reads as quiet text ("Sprint"), not a bordered pill, per the status-is-earned rule in the aesthetic north star.
  • Header carries the progress line (unchanged) and gets a faint surface tint so it reads as chrome, not as text floating on the artwork.

This is the small, ships-now fix. The larger direction (creation hub modal replacing the step wizard) is mocked separately in docs/design/2026-07-07-creation-hub-modal.html.
